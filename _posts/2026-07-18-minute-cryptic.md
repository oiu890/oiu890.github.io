# Hi
If you cant tell, I love playing minute cryptic :)  
Here are some cryptics that I've made! 😯😝😃  
And if you don't know what a minute cryptic is, do try out [minute cryptic](https://www.minutecryptic.com/)!!  
  
Essentially Minute Cryptic is a daily puzzle game that breaks cryptic crosswords into single-clue challenges.  
The goal is to figure out the right word using the clue  
A rough rundown would be:  
- you're given a sentence
- this sentence can contain the `fodder` (words/letters that you manipulate to get the answer), `indicators` (these tell you how to transform/use the fodder) and the `definition` (which is the definition of the word)  
- sometimes there could even be only definitions, only wordplay etc!  

It takes some practice to be able to start spotting all the indicators and figuring out how to manipulate them, so don't be discouraged!!!! (please)  
It is actually really fun 😎😎😎👍👍👍👍  
I hope you enjoy these :)

# "Uno, Dos, Tres turmoil after one Sierra becomes Delta. I got it? (10)"
<div class="cryptic-scribble"></div>
<div class="cryptic-input" data-answer="UNDERSTOOD"></div>
<details>
<summary><span style="color:plum">Show Indicator</span></summary>
The indicators are <span style="color:plum">turmoil</span>,<span style="color:plum">one</span>, and <span style="color:plum">becomes</span> one of them is an anagram indicator, one is a replacement indicator, and the one is a selection indicator. We will need to find suitable substitues for some fodder, which will replace something in another fodder.  
</details>
<details>
<summary><span style="color:khaki">Show Fodder</span></summary>
The fodder is <span style="color:khaki">Uno, Dos, Tres</span>, <span style="color:khaki">Sierra</span> and <span style="color:khaki">Delta</span>
</details>
<details>
<summary><span style="color:lightblue">Show Definition</span></summary>
The definition is <span style="color:lightblue">I got it</span>.  
</details>
<details>
<summary><span style="color:yellowgreen">Show Answer/Explanation</span></summary>
The answer is <span style="color:yellowgreen">UNDERSTOOD</span><br>
"Sierra" and "Delta" refer to "S" and "D" in the phonetic alphabet respectively.<br>
So our clue becomes "... after ONE S becomes D".<br>
As such, one of the "S"'s in "UNO, DOS, TRES" will be replaced with a "D".  
Our "Turmoil" tells us to anagram this fodder, giving us "UNDERSTOOD".<br>
When you "get" something, you have understood it.
